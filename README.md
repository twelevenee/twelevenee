### AI student interested in language, society, and what technology does to people.

---

I study Artificial Intelligence at Sungkyunkwan University, with a focus on NLP and LLMs.

I am less interested in technology as a spectacle, and more interested in how it changes the way people understand information, make decisions, and relate to one another.

My work often sits between language AI and human-centered questions: fairness, accessibility, loneliness, public understanding, and the social effects of intelligent systems.

## Work

- Built an AI chatbot for the Corning AI Challenge to analyze HR survey data through natural language interaction.
- Developed an LLM-based platform that helps people understand political data more clearly and accessibly.

## Interests

- NLP and LLMs
- Human-centered AI
- Information accessibility
- AI ethics and society
- Poetry, humanities, and social questions

## Notes

I write poems, think often about loneliness and inequality, and care about building technology with human sensitivity.

Outside of work, I like quiet walks by the sea or in parks, reading in sunny cafes, tennis, and travel.
